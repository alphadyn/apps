# Fund Map

Fund Map is a companion to [Market Map](../market-map/): a viewport-filling, searchable treemap of the curated index mutual funds and ETFs in [Market Insights](../market-insights/). Tiles are grouped by exposure and sized by reported fund net assets. Vanguard mutual-fund and ETF share classes in the same pooled portfolio are combined into a single tile so the shared assets are not counted twice. Search still highlights each ticker; a shared tile links to details for each share class. Hover or keyboard-focus for the readout, select a tile for details, and press Enter in search to open the first match. Funds without net assets are excluded from their own tile but remain searchable and accessible through coverage. If a share class is part of a pooled portfolio with a reported asset value for another class, it remains included in the shared tile. The header icon reloads the app.

## Run

Serve the repository root with `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open <http://localhost:8000/fund-map/>. The app uses the Market Insights snapshot, generated by the existing Pages workflow; see [Market Insights setup](../market-insights/README.md) if it is missing. The workflow runs `python3 fund-map/build_assets.py` to refresh `assets.json` from Yahoo Finance fund pages and `python3 fund-map/build_holdings.py` to refresh reported holdings for ITOT, IVV, SPY, and SPYM. The holdings builder uses iShares CSV files and State Street XLSX files, and retains the prior snapshot for a source if that issuer temporarily cannot be reached. No additional key or dependency is required. The snapshots record fetch and holdings dates; Refresh reloads snapshots rather than fetching new assets or holdings. On load and Refresh, the existing quote service fetches Yahoo Finance quotes and Nasdaq company market caps. Snapshot stock prices and caps are cleared before fetching and are not used as fallback. The page shows incomplete-data errors and leaves unavailable estimates gray. Quotes may be delayed; mutual fund NAVs typically update once daily.

Yahoo's reported net assets can be for an entire fund rather than an individual share class. The known Vanguard pairs VFIAX/VOO, VTSAX/VTI, VTIAX/VXUS, and VBTLX/BND are therefore represented by one shared tile each, using their common pooled asset value. The tile does not imply a per-share-class allocation. Other funds can still have overlapping assets, so the map must not be interpreted as a non-overlapping total market. These are reported net assets, not ETF market capitalization. No fund asset values are fabricated or scaled to live prices.

## Proxy methodology

SPY, IVV, and SPYM use their respective issuer-reported holdings weights with the Market Insights earnings-based DCF. Each fund's implied upside is the weighted mean for reported holdings with usable live caps, prices, and positive earnings, normalized over covered holdings. The estimate is withheld unless covered holdings represent at least 80% of reported fund weight and holdings are no more than 45 days old. These funds track the same index, so results are expected to be similar, but their reported weights can produce different estimates.

ITOT uses [iShares' published holdings](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239724/x/latest-holdings.csv) with the same weighted method. Weights are rounded and dated, and are not updated with live prices. Non-S&P companies, cash and other positions, companies without usable DCFs, fund fees, and changes since the holdings date are excluded; their omission can materially bias the result. FXAIX and SWPPX currently use the shared S&P 500 proxy because supported issuer holdings feeds are not available in the automated build. Vanguard VFIAX/VOO share one pooled portfolio and therefore share the same proxy estimate. Other unsupported funds remain gray until suitable holdings and valuation data are available.

The estimate is withheld unless at least 80% of proxy companies have live caps and at least 80% of the observed live-cap total has a usable DCF. Coverage displays both ratios; the weight of missing caps is unknown. Missing or non-positive earnings are excluded rather than valued at zero and may materially bias the result.

The shared S&P 500 proxy is **not a valuation of actual fund holdings**. It uses company-wide capitalization rather than fund holdings weights, and does not model fund fees, cash, distributions, tracking error, or non-S&P assets. Holdings-based estimates model only covered holdings. The shared model projects five years of net income with growth capped at +/-5%, a 10% discount rate, and 2.5% terminal growth; it is not a full free-cash-flow model, a price forecast, or investment advice.

## Link previews

Share <https://alphadyn.github.io/apps/fund-map/>. Static Open Graph and Twitter metadata provide link crawlers with a title, description, and [preview image](social-preview.png) without running JavaScript. The artwork is illustrative, not current fund data. To regenerate it, install Pillow and run `python3 fund-map/generate_social_preview.py`. The page and image must be deployed before the production link can unfurl; preview support and caching depend on the sharing client.
