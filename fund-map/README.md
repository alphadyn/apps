# Fund Map

**Live app:** [Open Fund Map](https://alphadyn.github.io/apps/fund-map/)

> Part of [Alphadyn Apps](../README.md) · [Browse the catalog](../index.html)

Fund Map is a companion to [Market Map](../market-map/): a viewport-filling, searchable treemap of the curated index mutual funds and ETFs in [Market Insights](../market-insights/). Tiles are grouped by exposure and sized by reported fund net assets. Vanguard mutual-fund and ETF share classes in the same pooled portfolio are combined into a single tile so the shared assets are not counted twice. Search still highlights each ticker; a shared tile links to details for each share class. Hover or keyboard-focus for the readout, select a tile for details, and press Enter in search to open the first match. Funds without net assets are excluded from their own tile but remain searchable and accessible through coverage. If a share class is part of a pooled portfolio with a reported asset value for another class, it remains included in the shared tile. The header icon reloads the app.

## Run

Serve the repository root with `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open <http://localhost:8000/fund-map/>. The app uses the Market Insights snapshot, generated by the existing Pages workflow; see [Market Insights setup](../market-insights/README.md) if it is missing. The workflow runs `python3 fund-map/build_assets.py` to refresh `assets.json` from Yahoo Finance fund pages and `python3 fund-map/build_holdings.py` to refresh fund holdings. The builder uses SEC EDGAR N-PORT for FXAIX, FSKAX, SWPPX, SWTSX, and SCHB; Vanguard's published portfolio API for VFIAX/VOO, VTSAX/VTI, VTIAX/VXUS and VBTLX/BND; iShares CSV files for ITOT and IVV; and State Street XLSX files for SPY and SPYM. If a source is temporarily unavailable, its previous snapshot is retained. No additional key or dependency is required. The snapshots record fetch and holdings dates; Refresh reloads snapshots rather than fetching new assets or holdings. On load and Refresh, the existing quote service fetches Yahoo Finance quotes and Nasdaq company market caps. Snapshot stock prices and caps are cleared before fetching and are not used as fallback. The page shows incomplete-data errors and leaves unavailable estimates gray. Quotes may be delayed; mutual fund NAVs typically update once daily.

Yahoo's reported net assets can be for an entire fund rather than an individual share class. The known Vanguard pairs VFIAX/VOO, VTSAX/VTI, VTIAX/VXUS, and VBTLX/BND are therefore represented by one shared tile each, using their common pooled asset value. The tile does not imply a per-share-class allocation. Other funds can still have overlapping assets, so the map must not be interpreted as a non-overlapping total market. These are reported net assets, not ETF market capitalization. No fund asset values are fabricated or scaled to live prices.

## Estimation methodology

Each equity fund's implied upside is the weighted mean of its reported holdings' Market Insights earnings-based DCF upside, normalized over holdings with usable live caps, prices, and positive earnings. The estimate is withheld unless covered holdings represent at least 80% of fund assets and issuer holdings are no more than 45 days old. SEC N-PORT reports are delayed and may be up to 120 days old. No index proxy is substituted for missing holdings or insufficient coverage. Holdings weights are dated and not updated with live prices; estimates for funds tracking the same index can be similar even though they use different portfolios.

Vanguard share classes use the same reported pooled portfolio. Vanguard reports rounded percentage weights, so some positions have a zero weight and the reported weights may not sum to 100%; the model does not invent the missing weight. SEC equities are matched to tracked securities using Vanguard's reported CUSIP-to-ticker mapping, explicit mappings for certain share classes, or unique normalized issuer names. Unmapped holdings, cash and other assets, companies without usable DCFs, fund fees, and changes since the report date are excluded from the estimate; unmapped positions still count against the 80% coverage threshold. Their omission can materially bias the estimate.

Market Insights currently covers S&P 500 companies only. International holdings are not matched by ticker alone because tickers are not globally unique; VTIAX/VXUS therefore remain unavailable until reliable international security identifiers and valuation inputs are available. Vanguard's bond portfolio includes positions with uncertain cash flows, including mortgage-backed securities and short positions, and does not provide bond credit ratings. VBTLX/BND remain unavailable until a bond cash-flow model, credit-quality inputs and independent yield curves are available; company-earnings DCF is never applied to bonds.

Holdings-based estimates model only covered positions. SEC N-PORT reports can be older than issuer holdings and may not reflect subsequent portfolio changes. The equity model projects five years of net income with growth capped at +/-5%, a 10% discount rate, and 2.5% terminal growth; it is not a full free-cash-flow model, a price forecast, or investment advice.

## Link previews

Share <https://alphadyn.github.io/apps/fund-map/>. Static Open Graph and Twitter metadata provide link crawlers with a title, description, and [preview image](social-preview.png) without running JavaScript. The artwork is illustrative, not current fund data. To regenerate it, install Pillow and run `python3 fund-map/generate_social_preview.py`. The page and image must be deployed before the production link can unfurl; preview support and caching depend on the sharing client.
