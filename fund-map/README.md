# Fund Map

Fund Map is a companion to [Market Map](../market-map/): a viewport-filling, searchable treemap of the curated index mutual funds and ETFs in [Market Insights](../market-insights/). Tiles are grouped by exposure and sized by reported fund net assets. Search highlights matches without changing areas; hover or keyboard-focus for the readout, select a tile for details, and press Enter in search to open the first match. Funds without net assets are excluded from the map but remain searchable and accessible through coverage. The header icon reloads the app.

## Run

Serve the repository root with `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open <http://localhost:8000/fund-map/>. The app uses the Market Insights snapshot, generated by the existing Pages workflow; see [Market Insights setup](../market-insights/README.md) if it is missing. The workflow also runs `python3 fund-map/build_assets.py` to refresh `assets.json` from Yahoo Finance fund pages and `python3 fund-map/build_holdings.py` to refresh the ITOT issuer holdings snapshot. No additional key or dependency is required. The net-assets snapshot records when it was fetched, not when the provider measured it; Refresh reloads snapshots rather than fetching new assets or holdings. On load and Refresh, the existing quote service fetches Yahoo Finance quotes and Nasdaq company market caps. Snapshot stock prices and caps are cleared before fetching and are not used as fallback. The page shows incomplete-data errors and leaves unavailable estimates gray. Quotes may be delayed; mutual fund NAVs typically update once daily.

Yahoo's reported net assets can be for an entire fund rather than an individual share class. Related tickers can therefore have the same or overlapping assets: tile sizes are not necessarily share-class assets, and the map must not be interpreted as a non-overlapping total market. These are reported net assets, not ETF market capitalization. No fund asset values are fabricated or scaled to live prices.

## Proxy methodology

The curated S&P 500 funds use the existing Market Map issuer grouping and Market Insights earnings-based DCF to calculate a **market-cap-weighted average implied upside** for S&P 500 companies with usable live caps, prices, and positive earnings. It normalizes over the covered companies, then multiplies each S&P 500 fund's live price or NAV by `1 + proxy upside` for an illustrative fair value. The same upside is applied to all these funds.

ITOT uses [iShares' published holdings](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239724/x/latest-holdings.csv) instead: for each reported equity position, the model matches the ticker to the Market Insights S&P 500 universe, multiplies that company's DCF upside by its reported holdings weight, and normalizes over matched positions with usable DCFs. The fund's price is multiplied by one plus this weighted upside. The estimate requires usable DCFs covering at least 80% of fund assets by reported weight and a holdings date no more than 45 days old. Weights are rounded and dated, and are not updated with live prices. Non-S&P companies, cash and other positions, companies without usable DCFs, fund fees, and changes since the holdings date are excluded; their omission can materially bias the result. See each fund's details for coverage and holdings date. Other funds remain gray until suitable holdings and valuation data are available.

The estimate is withheld unless at least 80% of proxy companies have live caps and at least 80% of the observed live-cap total has a usable DCF. Coverage displays both ratios; the weight of missing caps is unknown. Missing or non-positive earnings are excluded rather than valued at zero and may materially bias the result.

The S&P 500 proxy is **not a valuation of actual fund holdings**. It uses company-wide capitalization rather than float-adjusted index weights, and does not model actual fund holdings, fees, cash, distributions, tracking error, or non-S&P assets. The ITOT estimate models only covered holdings. The shared model projects five years of net income with growth capped at +/-5%, a 10% discount rate, and 2.5% terminal growth; it is not a full free-cash-flow model, a price forecast, or investment advice.

## Link previews

Share <https://alphadyn.github.io/apps/fund-map/>. Static Open Graph and Twitter metadata provide link crawlers with a title, description, and [preview image](social-preview.png) without running JavaScript. The artwork is illustrative, not current fund data. To regenerate it, install Pillow and run `python3 fund-map/generate_social_preview.py`. The page and image must be deployed before the production link can unfurl; preview support and caching depend on the sharing client.
