# User Guide

## Overview

Northstar EHR is a responsive clinical workflow specification for emergency and inpatient care. It covers chart review, patient intake, care-team assignment, encounter progression, orders, notes, appointments, follow-up, and billing context.

The public deployment is a browser-only functional prototype, not an approved system for clinical use. The built-in records are fictional, changes are stored in browser local storage, and the sign-in and Admin-managed roles are demonstrations rather than real authentication or server-enforced permissions. A deployed clinical system must replace these mechanisms with organization-managed identity, server-side authorization, protected clinical storage, audit controls, validated integrations, and approved operating procedures.

## Clinical-use requirements

Before this software is used for real care, the organization must approve and document the intended patient population, facilities, workforce roles, clinical privileges, data retention, privacy practices, interoperability scope, downtime procedures, incident response, and backup/recovery plan. A clinical deployment must provide:

- Organization-managed workforce identity, MFA, account lifecycle controls, and session revocation.
- Server-enforced least-privilege and patient-scope access for every read, write, search, export, signature, and image request.
- Protected clinical-record storage, tested encryption and key management, and private, authorized integrations.
- Tamper-resistant audit records, monitoring, access review, break-glass controls, and incident procedures.
- Clinically validated workflows, appropriate professional sign-off and co-signature policies, medication/order safeguards, privacy review, security review, and required regulatory and contractual approvals.
- Approved clinical governance and vendor agreements, including BAAs where applicable.

Do not enter real patient information or make care decisions in the public prototype. Clinical use is authorized only after the organization has completed the release gates in [SECURITY_ARCHITECTURE.md](./SECURITY_ARCHITECTURE.md) and deployed the approved backend and operating controls.

## Sign-in and roles

On first load, **Joe Spartan, MD** is automatically signed in with the **Provider** role only. His sign-in persists in this browser across refreshes and browser restarts until explicit sign-out. Signing out disables automatic sign-in, including after reload; use `joe.spartan` with password `northstar` to sign in again and re-enable persistence. Existing sessions for other sample accounts are not replaced; those accounts retain tab-local sessions.

After sign-in, a warning screen consolidates the fictional-data, clinical-use, live-service, security, and browser-storage limitations. Select **I understand - continue** to open Overview, or **Sign out** to return to sign-in. Acknowledgment lasts only for that tab's signed-in session, so refreshing does not repeat the warning; reopening in a new tab or after a browser restart requires acknowledgment again. Signing out clears acknowledgment but does not clear browser-stored patient data. Context-specific reference-image labels and directory/account guidance remain beside the relevant content.

Only an account with an active **Admin** role can manage sign-in account authorizations from **Overview > Account roles**. Check one or more roles (Provider, Midlevel (PA / NP), Nurse, Staff, or Admin) and select **Save roles**. Each account must have at least one authorized role, and the app prevents removing the last authorized Admin. Authorizations persist across sign-out and reload in the same browser. Directory-only staff profiles do not have sign-in accounts.

Each sign-in account has its own role card. Save confirmation and validation messages appear in the Operations overview, including when an authorization change removes your active Admin role.

Use **Active role** in the sidebar to switch among your account's authorized roles. Only the active role's capabilities apply; permissions are not combined. The selector is disabled if you have only one role. The active role persists on reload. If an Admin revokes your active role, the app selects the first remaining authorized role when the account is next loaded or updated. An account authorized for Admin can switch back to Admin to manage authorizations.

Provider and Midlevel roles support encounter, order, note, task, and data-exchange workflows. Nurse retains nursing workflow, order, note, appointment, and task capabilities. Staff supports appointment completion and chart selection; Admin supports account management, administrative review, billing, and data exchange. These controls are demo UI permissions, not trusted access control. Sample sign-ins use `elena.ruiz` (Provider and Admin, initially active as Provider), `jordan.lee` (Nurse), or `alex.morgan` (Admin), with the password `northstar`. Saved Admin-managed authorizations take precedence over sample defaults; existing accounts can be updated from Account roles. The sidebar distinguishes authorized roles from the active role. Do not enter or import real patient information.

## Patient care

Open **Patient care > Patient care home** to choose a workflow. Use the patient search in the workspace header to find a patient by name, MRN, diagnosis, care team, or location; selecting a result updates the patient in context without leaving a patient workflow. The context area shows the patient's MRN, demographics, unit, location, diagnosis, allergies, encounter status, disposition, and provider. Choose a workflow to show its details below the patient context on the same screen. The workflow list collapses to show the current selection; expand **Patient workflows** to choose another. The selected workflow remains visible after a refresh. The patient's chart, profile, encounter, orders, results, medication list, formulary, outpatient care, appointments, documents, or billing are available. The Patient care menu displays the current patient's MRN while the patient is in context. Outpatient appointments are scheduled for the patient in context.

If an order has unsaved changes, changing patients asks for confirmation before clearing the order draft. Cancel to keep both the current patient and draft. Order drafts themselves are not saved across a browser refresh.

## Patient chart

The standalone worklist contains 23 fictional charts spanning a variety of conditions and histories. Choose a patient or filter the worklist by name, MRN, diagnosis, priority, or status. Selecting a patient updates and focuses the chart and brings the top of its card into view. The header has a patient search; the Staff directory has a separate search for staff by name and profile details. Selecting a patient from outside a patient workflow opens their profile; within a patient workflow it updates the patient in context. At the top of the chart, the medical-status dashboard summarizes the primary diagnosis, encounter stage, department/location, attending, assigned nurse, priority, and allergies. The vital dashboard displays the latest charted blood pressure, heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature, oxygen saturation, and pain score; when repeat readings are recorded, expand **View recent readings** to review their timestamps and values. The imaging dashboard shows study and report counts, preliminary reports, open imaging orders, and the latest report summary.

The **Patient timeline** brings together diagnoses, lab markers, clinical documents, orders, imaging studies, appointments, vital recordings, completed and open care tasks, and encounter updates. Events are grouped as **Previous**, **Current**, **Planned**, or **Date not recorded**. When an admission timestamp is available, the timeline starts at admission and excludes earlier dated events; when it is unavailable, the timeline shows all available chart history and says so rather than inventing a date. Undated events are retained but cannot be placed relative to admission. Select an event to expand its details, then choose **Open record** to jump to the matching chart record or workflow. New admissions record their start time when the encounter first transitions to Admission or an admit disposition.

When opening **Formulary** from a patient's workflow, the selected patient and navigation remain visible so you can return to orders or another patient workspace. The sidebar **Formulary** remains available for independent medication searches.

## Encounter workflow

To register a new fictional patient, choose **Register patient** in **Patients & care > Worklist & charts**, or choose **Register a patient** from **Admissions**. Provider, Midlevel, and Nurse roles can enter a name, unique MRN, age, and sex, then select **Create profile & open triage**. Check the worklist for an existing record first; duplicate MRNs are rejected. The profile is saved locally and opens the new patient's intake form. No admission, diagnosis, allergy, medication, or vital assessment is inferred by registration. The admissions census includes the patient only after an admission stage or admit disposition is saved.

Use **Cancel** to close the registration form without creating a profile; its unsaved entries remain available until the form is reset or the page is reloaded.

When a patient is in context, use the **Patient care** menu to move between that patient's chart, encounter, orders, results, appointments, and medications. The patient's MRN appears beside the menu label. In patient workflow panels, use the patient selector to change context without leaving the current workspace.

Open **ED & inpatient** to document an encounter. The form groups arrival and triage separately from care-team and disposition details:

1. Record arrival mode, encounter stage, chief complaint, acuity, triage observations, and intake vitals.
2. Update the department, care location, attending provider, assigned nurse, and disposition as the patient moves through ED evaluation, consultation, admission, discharge, or transfer.
3. For ward/ICU admission or PCP/specialist referral, record the receiving location or destination and follow-up date.
4. Use **Orders** to track nursing, vitals, diagnosis, H&P, laboratory, imaging/radiology, procedure, medication, consult, and referral requests. Expand **New order**, choose an **Order type**, and complete its structured fields. Unsaved fields are kept separately when switching categories, but cleared when changing patients or reloading. For a surgical operation, choose **Procedure** and use the surgical catalog filter. For catalog-based prescriptions, the medication choice links to **Medications & prescriptions**; general medication requests can still be entered here. Save the order to open its optional scheduling form, or schedule it later from the saved order. Expand **Order details** on a saved request to review its structured fields. Pending orders can be acknowledged, completed, or cancelled in the demo.
   For a procedure or surgical operation, search the illustrative catalog by name or specialty. Use **Catalog filter** to show surgical operations, browse the result pages, and select an entry to fill the order and procedure names. Complete the required details and add the order, or enter a procedure manually. The catalog is not a validated clinical order set or billing terminology; orders are not transmitted or performed.
   For an imaging/radiology order, search the radiology catalog by study name or modality and select a result to fill the request and modality, or enter a study manually. Review the contrast selection, specify the site and laterality when applicable, and complete the indication and other required fields. The sidebar **Radiology studies & procedures** catalog also supports searching without a patient; expand a result and select a unique patient by name or MRN to open a prefilled order. The catalog contains illustrative names, not validated protocols, clinical guidance, or billing terminology.
   The sidebar **Procedures & operations** catalog can also be searched without selecting a patient. Enter a name, specialty, or procedure type to see results; the full list is not shown initially. Expand a result to review its available name, specialty, and type. Clinical descriptions and risks are not supplied. Enter a unique patient name or MRN and choose **Select for patient order** to open that patient's prefilled order. From a patient chart, use **Order and schedule care** shortcuts for labs, radiology, procedures, operations, and follow-up. After saving an order, expand **Schedule this order** and enter a date, time, provider, and optional location. The appointment appears in the patient's schedule and links back to the order. Cancelling or completing an order and its appointment are separate actions; cancel an existing appointment before scheduling the order again. These appointments do not reserve real services.
5. Use **Notes & documents** to review saved documents or expand **New document** to create drafts for ED provider, history and physical, admission, SOAP progress, consultation, nursing, procedure, discharge summary, disposition, and referral notes. Each template provides documentation fields for its workflow, such as a problem-based admission plan, SOAP sections, procedure consent and findings, discharge instructions and return precautions, or referral question and destination.

Saved orders retain and display their type-specific details. Saved documents retain and display each completed template field. Saved documents remain drafts; the demo does not implement authentication, co-signature, or legally valid electronic signatures.

## Other views

- **Outpatient care** schedules and manages selected-patient ambulatory visits with date, time, visit type, provider, location, reason for visit, check-in, completion, and cancellation controls. Availability and completion are stored with the patient's local demo chart; they do not create referrals or real clinical bookings.
- **My patients** shows patients whose assigned nurse (Nurse role) or attending (Provider or Midlevel role) matches the signed-in account's linked staff profile. Each card shows care-team assignments, open care-task count, and pending or acknowledged patient orders; use **Open chart** or **View order** to continue the existing workflows. Orders are for the assigned patients and are not filtered by ordering clinician. Update assignments in **ED & inpatient**. If no patients or linked profile match, the view explains why; Admin and Staff roles can switch to an authorized clinical role. This is a local demo, not an assignment or notification service.
- **In Basket** groups fictional care-team messages, sample results, and care-plan follow-up tasks. Filter by type or search by patient, MRN, subject, or detail. Mark messages read, mark results reviewed, or complete eligible tasks; task completion also updates the matching sample care-plan task. The unread count appears in the workspace navigation. These are local demonstration entries only—not a real messaging, result-routing, or clinical-review system.
- **Overview** summarizes patient/chart activity and aggregate admissions, departments, active team members, and open orders without displaying individual charts. Metric cards open a filtered patient worklist. Admins also see account-role management and the activity audit trail. **Patients > Admissions** provides the full census, with department, location, attending, nurse, stage, diagnosis, and chart links.
- **Nursing station** filters patients by assigned nurse and shows their location, diagnosis, priority/stage, latest charted vitals, and allergy list. Update nurse assignments in **ED & inpatient**.
- **Departments** lists ED, MICU, SICU, NICU, radiology, surgery, OB/GYN, pediatrics, neurology, telemetry, and medicine, along with sample team and patient counts. **Locations** lists care areas and illustrative occupancy.
- **Staff directory** contains fictional administrator, provider, PA, nurse, and midlevel profiles. Search the directory by name, role, department, location, or email, then select **View profile** on a matching card. Physician names use the **Name, MD** format in directory cards and staff profiles, including the profile selector and portrait labels. Existing care-team assignments are unchanged. The Admin demo role can add directory-only staff profiles; this does not create an account. **Patients > Patient profiles** displays patient context and links to the full chart.
- Use the patient header search to find patients by name, MRN, diagnosis, care team, location, or department. In Staff directory, use the separate search to find staff by name, role, department, location, or demo email; results always open staff profiles. The patient worklist has its own filter; the patient list is independently scrollable.
- In the ED & inpatient workflow, use the care-team search fields to narrow the attending and nurse assignment lists. Searching does not change the selected assignment; save the workflow update after choosing a different team member.
- The sidebar groups **Overview** and **In Basket** under Your workspace, clinical views under **Patients & care**, directories under Organization, and Billing under Administration. Icons and a highlighted current page help orient navigation; patient workflows expand their menu automatically. Use the header's **Hide navigation / Show navigation** button to collapse or restore the sidebar; the desktop preference is remembered in this browser. On smaller screens, navigation opens as a drawer, closes after selecting a view, and supports Escape, backdrop dismissal, and keyboard focus containment.
- **Open encounter** stays at the top of navigation. The account card keeps your name and sign-out visible; expand **Role & permissions** to review authorizations or change the active role. Header **Data tools** contains the existing import/export format, scope, and actions without cluttering the workspace. It can be dismissed by clicking outside or pressing Escape.
- **Patients & care** groups patient management views separately from clinical tools. The **Worklist & charts** filter searches patient name, MRN, diagnosis, status, care team, and location; select a result to view its chart and current status summary.
- **Appointments** supports completing appointments; the demo admin role can cancel them.
- **Labs & medications** displays chart data and supports the existing encounter form.
- **Billing** displays the selected patient's sample balance, insurance, claim status, and last payment immediately. Use the header patient search to see another patient's billing context. Appointment tools and the Admin audit trail remain available in their respective views.
- The active Admin demo role can review the activity audit trail and manage authorized roles. Other users can switch among their authorized roles, but cannot grant themselves additional roles.

## Persistence and reset

Changes are saved to local storage in the current browser profile and remain after refresh. Clearing this site's browser storage removes the demo changes. There is no server synchronization, backup, or multi-user workflow.

## Safety and privacy

Do not enter, import, or export real patient information. All patient histories and imaging reports are fictional illustrative examples. This prototype is not a clinical system and must not be used to guide or document patient care. Its orders are tracking examples only: they are not transmitted, validated, or executed, and medication entries are not prescriptions. The proposed—but unimplemented—production security design is in [SECURITY_ARCHITECTURE.md](./SECURITY_ARCHITECTURE.md). Production requires organizational risk analysis, approved secure infrastructure and identity/access controls, validated clinical and medication workflows, interoperability, applicable BAAs, and privacy, security, safety, legal, and regulatory review.
