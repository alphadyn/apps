Before you continue

Demonstration environment

  • Fictional data only. Patient records and results are fictional. Do not enter real patient information.
  • Not for clinical use. Do not use this app for clinical decisions or as diagnostic guidance.
  • No live services. Orders, notes, prescriptions, messages, and results stay in the demo and are never sent or executed.
  • Medication catalog limitations. Examples only; not live inventory or verified prescribing or safety guidance.
  • Not a secure clinical system. Sign-in is browser-local, not real authentication. No HIPAA safeguards are provided.
  • Browser-local storage. Changes are not protected, backed up, or shared. Signing out does not clear stored data.
