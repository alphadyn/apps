Before you continue

Demonstration environment

Review these important limitations before entering Northstar EHR.

  • Fictional data only. Patient records, staff profiles, messages, results, reports, and capacity values are fictional or illustrative. Do not enter, import, or export real patient information.
  • Not for clinical use. Do not use this app to make or document clinical decisions. Reports are not diagnostic, and results are not clinically interpreted. Reference images are educational examples, not patient scans.
  • No live clinical services. Orders, notes, prescriptions, messages, and results are not sent to clinical systems or care teams. Documents remain drafts without legally valid electronic signatures.
  • Not a secure clinical system. Sign-in and roles are browser-local demonstrations, not real authentication or server-enforced authorization. Profiles are not verified identities or access credentials. This app does not provide HIPAA safeguards.
  • Browser-local storage. Changes stay in this browser; they are not protected, backed up, or shared with a care team. Joe Spartan's automatic sign-in persists until explicit sign-out. Signing out disables automatic sign-in but does not clear or protect stored data.
