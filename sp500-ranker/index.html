Methodology & data sources

How each figure is calculated
  • Constituents come from the public S&P 500 list; multi-class issuers (e.g. GOOGL/GOOG) appear once per listed security.
  • Price is the latest close; 1Y and 3M growth compare it with the split-adjusted close one year and three months earlier (excluding dividends).
  • Market cap is Nasdaq's issuer-level figure re-based to the latest close. YoY market cap growth combines 1-year price growth with the change in split-restated weighted diluted shares from SEC filings.
  • Net income is trailing twelve months attributable to the company, built from SEC 10-Q/10-K filings (fiscal year when quarterly data is incomplete). Growth against a negative base is measured relative to its absolute value.
  • Price history uses daily closes for the last two years and weekly closes before that.
  • Missing values (shown as —) always rank last. Summary figures reflect the selected sector.

Data: S&P 500 constituents list, Nasdaq, Yahoo Finance, SEC EDGAR. Refreshed with each site deployment.
